How HaloDrops handles your files, local settings, and information you choose to share.

Your files stay on your Mac.

This policy describes how HaloDrops for macOS and its website handle information. HaloDrops is developed by Mehraab Singh. The app converts and edits files locally; it does not upload your files to a conversion server or require a HaloDrops account.

HaloDrops does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. It does not sell your personal information.

Information used on your device

Files and metadata. HaloDrops reads files you select, open, or drag into its tools, including the contents and metadata needed for the requested operation. Converted and edited files are saved on your Mac. Some operations create temporary working files and attempt to remove them when the operation finishes. Output files remain until you delete them.

Drag gestures and clipboard actions. The app observes mouse and modifier-key events and the drag pasteboard to show the conversion wheel. File actions can copy files, paths, or metadata to the system clipboard when you choose them. Other apps may access clipboard content subject to macOS controls.

Preferences. Appearance, output destinations, excluded applications, conversion settings, a configured FFmpeg path, and update-check state are stored locally using macOS preferences.

Optional diagnostics. Debug logging is disabled by default. If explicitly enabled for troubleshooting, local logs may contain filenames, paths, operation details, errors, and drag-event information. These logs are not automatically sent to the developer.

Files saved in a folder managed by iCloud or another sync or backup service may be transferred by that service under its own settings. HaloDrops does not control those services.

Update checks and external services

The current app checks GitHub for release information at launch and periodically while running. These requests go to the Open-Tangerine release endpoint on GitHub and include an app-identifying User-Agent. GitHub receives your IP address and ordinary connection information. Your selected files and their contents are not included in these checks.

Choosing the website update command opens the Open-Tangerine download website in your browser with the installed app version in the URL. Opening developer or support links also connects to the relevant external service.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising scripts, or tracking cookies. GitHub states that Pages visitors’ IP addresses are logged for security purposes. See GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of connection, account, and support data.

Support information you share

If you submit a GitHub support issue, the developer receives your GitHub profile name and any information you choose to include, such as your app version, problem description, or attachments. This information is used to understand and respond to your request.

Issues and comments in the support repository are public and may remain available after a request is resolved. Do not post private files, credentials, sensitive personal information, or unredacted logs. GitHub manages storage and account data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices and requests

You control which files the app processes, where output is saved, and whether to share support information. Local files and preferences stay on your device unless you or a service you use share them. See User privacy choices for practical controls, deletion information, and how to request help with information you have shared.

For questions about this policy, contact Mehraab Singh through HaloDrops support. Because the support channel is public, start with a general request and do not include sensitive details. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you provided to the developer; requests involving GitHub-held data may also need to go to GitHub.

Policy updates

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date. This policy describes the current app and website; external services maintain their own policies.
