User privacy choices

Practical controls for your files, app activity, and support information.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Choose what you process.

HaloDrops processes files locally without a HaloDrops account. Select only the files you want to use. Set the output location in Settings → General → Output. If you do not want outputs synced to a cloud service, choose a location that is not managed by that service.

You can delete original and output files in Finder whenever you choose. Uninstalling HaloDrops does not automatically delete the files you created. Temporary files may also remain after an interrupted operation.

Control app activity.

  • Use the excluded-applications setting to prevent the conversion wheel from appearing in selected apps.
  • Turn off Start automatically at login in HaloDrops Settings if you do not want the app to launch when you sign in.
  • Review any permissions granted to HaloDrops in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Available controls depend on your macOS version.
  • Quit HaloDrops from its menu bar menu to stop its drag monitoring and background update checks.

The current app does not have an in-app switch for automatic update checks. Local conversion tools can work without an internet connection; website links and update checks require a connection.

Keep diagnostics private.

Debug logs are off by default and are not automatically uploaded. If you enabled diagnostic logging for troubleshooting, remove that debug configuration and relaunch the app to stop logging. Use Show Logs to locate the diagnostic log when present, then quit the app before deleting it in Finder. Separately enabled drag-debug logs may be stored in a different location.

Before sharing a log or screenshot, remove filenames, paths, document content, or other information you do not want to make public. Copying file paths or metadata through the app puts that information on your Mac’s clipboard; copying something else replaces the current clipboard content, although clipboard-history tools may retain earlier copies.

Manage information you shared.

Submitting a support request is optional. GitHub issues and comments are public. You can edit your contributions using GitHub’s available controls, but editing or deleting visible content may not remove cached copies, edit history, or copies made by others.

To ask about access, correction, or deletion of information you supplied to the developer, open a general privacy support request. Do not include the sensitive information itself. For information held by GitHub, use the contact and privacy-request options in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

There is no HaloDrops account to delete and no advertising or analytics opt-in to manage. Local preferences can remain after uninstalling the app; contact support for help locating the correct app data before removing it.
